What to show (capability mapped): A concrete coexistence architecture diagram: Snowflake deployed in your Azure region, ingesting from the existing estate; ring-fenced estates in separate accounts/roles; private connectivity, customer-managed keys, and Horizon governance across the top. Objections & responses

If they push back…Respond
Why add a platform to my Microsoft estate?It's not additive — it consolidates seven fragmented risk systems into one governed platform running on Azure, so you reduce sprawl and finally get cross-estate lineage. Fewer moving parts, not more.
This becomes a political fight with Microsoft.Coexistence avoids that: Azure stays where it earns its keep, Snowflake is the neutral governed source of truth over the estate, deployed in Azure. You approve on documented grounds, not a vendor war.
Why not just use Azure-native (Fabric/Synapse) or Databricks?Fabric/Synapse keep you inside the same fragmented, per-estate pattern that failed the audit; Databricks leads with the lakehouse but governance and cross-estate lineage are add-ons rather than first-class. Snowflake's differentiator here is governed lineage across both estates as a native capability — to validate against your own test cases.

Carry forward

Marcus's security non-negotiables and evidence bar define the funded-first-step (POC) scope. His coexistence boundary is the backbone of the CFO 'Going Wide' story. Feed his sequencing need back to Charlotte.
